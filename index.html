Privacy Policy – Madarekbaan

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Madarekbaan is a personal document and card management application designed to help users store, organize, and manage their personal documents, identification cards, bank cards, and expiration dates. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, information may include:

  • Personal information entered by the user.
  • Document and identification card details.
  • Bank card information entered by the user.
  • Images and photos of personal documents.
  • Expiration dates and reminder settings.
  • Google account information when using Google sign-in.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information is used to provide application features, including document storage, organization, expiration reminders, backup, restoration, and synchronization with Google Drive when authorized by the user.

4. Data Storage and Backup

Madarekbaan supports local data storage on the user's device. Users may optionally connect their Google account to enable cloud backup and restore features. Google Drive access is used for these features according to the permissions granted by the user.

5. Google Account and Third-Party Services

If users choose Google sign-in or Google Drive backup, certain information may be processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy:

Google Privacy Policy

We do not sell users' personal information to third parties.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices and Google accounts.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties for advertising purposes. Information may be processed by Google services when users voluntarily enable Google sign-in or cloud backup.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can manage locally stored information through the App. Cloud backup data can be managed or deleted through Google Drive. Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored application data depending on device and Android settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Madarekbaan is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through a separate data collection process.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
